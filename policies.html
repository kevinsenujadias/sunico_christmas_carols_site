Terms & Conditions

This website sells seated tickets for Winter Vespers, the SUNICO Christmas carol evening at Namal Hall on Friday 18 December 2026, from 4:30 PM. By booking a ticket you agree to these terms.

Tickets and seats

  • Each ticket admits one person to the seat printed on the ticket. Seats are allocated at the time of booking.
  • Prices are shown in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) and include all charges. Prices: VIP Rs 5,000 · Gold Rs 3,500 · Silver Rs 2,500 · Bronze Rs 1,500.
  • A maximum of 10 seats can be booked in one booking.
  • A booking is confirmed only after payment is successfully received. Seats are held for a short time while you pay and are released if payment is not completed.

Entry

  • Every ticket has its own QR code. The QR code is scanned at the door and can be used only once. Please keep your tickets private, a copied ticket that has already been scanned will be refused.
  • Please arrive early. Late arrivals may be seated at a suitable break in the programme.
  • The organisers may refuse entry for tickets that are duplicated, altered or not purchased through this website or the school office.
  • Tickets may not be resold for profit.

Changes to the event

The organisers may make reasonable changes to the programme or performers. If the event is cancelled or moved to another date, the Refund & Cancellation policy below applies.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Tickets are non-refundable once purchased, except in the situations listed below.
  • Event cancelled: you will receive a full refund of the ticket price.
  • Event postponed: your tickets remain valid for the new date. If you cannot attend the new date, you may request a full refund within 7 days of the new date being announced.
  • Payment taken but no ticket issued, or charged twice for the same booking: the extra or failed payment will be refunded in full.
  • Tickets cannot be exchanged for a different seat or tier after purchase, unless the organisers need to move your seat, in which case you will be given a seat of the same tier or better.

How refunds are made

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method (the same card or account used to pay) through our payment provider, PayHere, normally within 14 working days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show the refund. To request a refund, email us with your booking number (e.g. WV-7K3QX9).

Ticket Delivery

Tickets are electronic (e-tickets). Nothing is posted. As soon as your payment is confirmed:

  • your tickets with QR codes appear immediately on the website under My Tickets, and
  • a confirmation email with your tickets is sent to the email address you entered.

You can open your tickets again on any phone from My Tickets → Find my tickets, using your booking number and phone number. Show the QR code on your phone (or a printout) at the door.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you book, we collect your name, phone number, email address, the seats you booked and your payment status.

How we use it

  • to issue and send your tickets,
  • to check tickets at the door, and
  • to contact you about your booking or any change to the event.

We do not sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.

Payments

Card payments are processed securely by PayHere. Your card details are entered on PayHere's secure payment page and are never seen or stored by this website.

How long we keep it

Booking details are kept until three months after the event (for any refund or enquiry) and are then deleted. You can ask us to delete your details earlier by emailing us. Tickets that are deleted can no longer be used for entry.

Cookies and storage

The website stores your tickets in your browser so you can open them again quickly. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
